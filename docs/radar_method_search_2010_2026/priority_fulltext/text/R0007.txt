Keywords:                                                 Although displacement measurements are critical in the health monitoring of civil infrastructure, accurate
Structural displacement measurement                       monitoring of structural displacements remains a challenge, especially for medium- and small-scale structures
Accelerometer                                             with only millimeter-scale vibrations. In this study, a high-precision displacement sensing system is developed by
Millimeter-wave radar
                                                          integrating millimeter-wave radar, accelerometer, and microprocessor unit (MPU). An automated and efficient
Data fusion
Edge computing
                                                          algorithm is proposed and applied for radar initial calibration, and then displacement is continuously estimated
                                                          by the built-in displacement sensing algorithm in the MPU. The developed displacement sensing system achieves
                                                          low cost (< $500), high accuracy (< 0.5 mm), and high sampling rate (100 Hz), and thus can be widely used in
                                                          medium- and small-scale structures. To validate the performance of the developed sensing system, a series of
                                                          field tests were conducted on nine structures in the USA, South Korea, and China. For all structures, the
                                                          developed sensing system was able to accurately measure displacements with a maximum root mean square error
                                                          of less than 0.06 mm, demonstrating the system’s potential for practical application in long-term continuous
                                                          displacement monitoring of medium- and small-scale structures.

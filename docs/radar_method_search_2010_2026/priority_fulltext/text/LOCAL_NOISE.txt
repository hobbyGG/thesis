   Abstract— Doppler radar uses phase demodulation to extract
the target displacement motion contained in the phase of the
echo signal. The demodulation performance is vulnerable to
the additive Gauss noise. Especially when the signal-to-noise
ratio (SNR) is extremely low in the scenarios of long-range
or subtle displacement motion sensing, it may be impossible to
extract the correct phase information. In this article, a phase
demodulation method with high robustness against additive noise
is proposed for accurate displacement motion reconstruction.
Mathematical derivations are performed to analyze the noise
effect on phase demodulation, and it shows that the influence of              Fig. 1. Simplified block diagram of the 24-GHz CW Doppler radar system

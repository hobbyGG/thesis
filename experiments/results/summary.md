# Simulation experiments

Generated by `python -m experiments.sweep report` from `ensemble.csv`, `sensitivity.csv` and `tuning.json`.

- Records: 4 s, 500 Hz radar frames, one train passage.
- Free parameters tuned on development seeds 1000-1009: `{"fusion_q": 10.0, "kf_only_q": 10000.0, "fixed_r": 0.0001, "fixed_r_q": 3.0, "complementary_corner_hz": 8.0, "adxl_hp_corner_hz": 0.3}`.
- Ensemble: 40 random scenes (seeds 6000-6039), each with its own structure, radar scene and sensor errors.
- Sensitivity: one factor at a time around the nominal scene, seeds 5000-5007.
- Error = estimate minus truth, both referenced to their first 0.2 s mean. Event RMSE covers the passage plus 0.3 s.
- Failure: non-finite estimate or maximum error above lambda/8 (about 0.49 mm, a 2*pi branch slip).
- Values are medians with the inter-quartile range in brackets; ratios clip RMSE to [0.01, 10000] um.
- Gains are paired reductions of the geometric-mean event RMSE (positive = fusion better) with a bootstrap 95% CI and a sign-flip p-value.

### 1.4 Engagement of the recursive circle fit

The fit accepts a circle only while the cumulative normal matrix has a condition number below 1e4, so a long static prefix with a short arc can keep it from engaging.

- Scenes whose median fitted |c|/R at the end of the record is zero (no accepted circle for most targets): 4/40 (seeds 6003, 6007, 6018, 6021).
- Scenes where fusion and no-circle are bit-identical (no accepted circle for any target): 2/40 (seeds 6003, 6018).

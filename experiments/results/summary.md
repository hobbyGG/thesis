# Simulation experiment results

Duration 4 s per trial (one train event at 0.5-1.3 s), 30 held-out seeds (2000-2029) per condition. Process noise tuned on seeds 1000-1009, separately per method: fusion q = 30, KF without ADXL q = 3000.
ADXL timestamps are exported as DRDY time minus the calibrated 3.26 ms group delay; the static axis offset (gravity projection and turn-on bias) is removed over the first 0.2 s, as in system.algorithm.io.
Cells are RMSE over the whole trial in um, mean±std over seeds; a trial fails when RMSE exceeds 6 % of the peak deflection. 'ADXL gain' is the reduction of the mean RMSE of fusion relative to the Kalman filter without ADXL.
